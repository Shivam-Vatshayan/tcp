Our Story

A family tandoor, now open for all of Blacktown

Tandoori Chicken Point started as a single clay tandoor and a family recipe carried from Punjab to Western Sydney. Today we still marinate every piece of chicken by hand, grind our own spice blends, and cook everything to order — the way it was always meant to be done.

No shortcuts, no microwaves, no pre-cooked trays sitting under a heat lamp. Just real fire, real spice, and a menu built around the dish that gave us our name.
